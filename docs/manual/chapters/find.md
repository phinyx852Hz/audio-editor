# The Find Panel and the Selector

[]{.ix t="Find panel"}The Find panel (`Ctrl+F`; pressed again it hides the panel) is PHI's browser, like Ableton Live's. One search field finds any process, Nyquist effect, plugin, instrument, visualization or audio file, and a sidebar narrows it down.

![The Find panel: the Selector colours, the categories and places, and the results.](images/find-panel.png)

## Searching

Type in the search field, and the results list everything that matches. In the sidebar:

- the **Selector** colours narrow it to your favourites of one colour;
- the **categories** (All, Processes, Effects, Plugins, Instruments, Visualizations, Files) narrow it to one kind of thing;
- **Places** narrow it to one folder; **Add Folder…** adds one.

`Enter` acts on the selected result: it runs a process, loads a plugin into the first free Master Section slot, opens an instrument, shows a visualization, or opens a file.

### Moving round the panel

| Keys | Does |
|---|---|
| `Down` from the empty search | Into the sidebar |
| `Down` after typing | Into the results |
| `Right` / `Left` | From the sidebar to the results, and back |
| `Up` at the top of a list | Back to the search |
| `Tab` | Between search, sidebar and results |
| `Enter` | Runs, loads or opens the result |
| `1` to `8` | Gives the result a Selector colour |
| `0` | Clears its colours |
| `Esc` | Clears the search, or returns to the editor |

## Browsing files

[]{.ix t="Find panel!files"}[]{.ix t="Auditioning files"}Files and Places are browsed one folder at a time; the header shows where you are, such as "Samples / Breaks / Jungle". `Enter` or `Right` opens a folder, and `Backspace`, `Left` or the `..` row goes up. Typing searches every folder at once.

**Auditioning.** Moving onto a file with the arrow keys, or clicking it, plays it without opening it, through the meters and after the Master Section. Clicking it again, or `K`, stops it.

From the file list:

- `Enter` opens the file in the editor (or, with the clip launcher showing, puts it in a clip slot; see [The Clip Launcher](#clip-launcher));
- `F7` and `F8` send it to Simplest or the Slicer, and leave the keyboard in Find, so you can arrow to the next file and try it;
- drag it onto the mixer, Simplest, the Slicer or the Analysis Meters window.

## The Selector {#selector}

[]{.ix t="Selector"}[]{.ix t="Favourites"}The Selector holds favourites in eight colours, from top to bottom: Silver, Violet, Indigo, Azure, Emerald, Gold, Saffron and Ruby. Give anything in Find a colour with `1` to `8`, and choose the colour in the sidebar to see just those.

![The Selector's sets, chosen with a right-click.](images/selector-sets.png)

### Selector sets

[]{.ix t="Selector!sets"}The Selector comes in sets, chosen with a right-click on the word Selector or on a colour. Each set has its own names for the eight colours and its own favourites:

| Set | Colour names, top to bottom |
|---|---|
| Favorites | Silver, Violet, Indigo, Azure, Emerald, Gold, Saffron, Ruby |
| Live Performance | FX, PD, ARP, VOX, LD, PR, BA, DR: the parts of a live set, from effects at the top to drums at the bottom |
| Mixing, Mastering, Podcast, Chakra | The colour names, until you give them names of your own |

`F2` or a double-click renames a colour, and the right-click menu renames the set.

### From the Selector to a mix

[]{.ix t="New Project from Selector Set"}Mixer > New Project from Selector Set starts a mix of eight tracks named and coloured from the chosen set, from the bottom up: for Live Performance, DR on track 1 up to FX on track 8. Each track then matches the favourites of its colour, which is what the [clip launcher](#clip-launcher) builds on.
