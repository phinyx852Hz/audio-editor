# Editing

## The editors compared

Five editors are the usual reference points for editing and mastering on a computer: MAGIX's
(now Boris FX's) **Sequoia**, Steinberg's **WaveLab**, **Adobe Audition**, **Sound Forge** and the
free **Audacity**. Beside them stand the specialists: **iZotope RX** for repair, **Pyramix** and
**SADiE** for classical, broadcast and high-resolution work, and the lighter **Acoustica** and
**Ocenaudio**.

WaveLab and Sequoia are the two dedicated mastering suites, WaveLab on Windows and macOS,
Sequoia on Windows only and much dearer, valued for album assembly and source-destination
editing. Audition is a waveform and multitrack editor aimed at post-production and video, sold
only by subscription. Sound Forge is a fast single-file editor for Windows. Audacity is the
leading free, open-source editor, on Windows, macOS and Linux.

| Editor | Maker | Platforms | Licence | Known for |

| WaveLab | Steinberg | Windows, macOS | Perpetual (Pro, Elements, Cast) | Mastering, DDP, loudness metering, batch |
| Audition | Adobe | Windows, macOS | Subscription (Creative Cloud) | Post-production, spectral editing, Premiere Pro link |
| Sound Forge | Boris FX | Windows | Perpetual or subscription | Fast destructive editing, CD burning |

| iZotope RX | Boris FX | Windows, macOS | Perpetual (Elements, Standard, Advanced) | Repair and restoration, spectral editing |

A few common claims need correcting. Sequoia has MIDI: it is built on Samplitude, a full
workstation, and keeps its MIDI editing. Audition had CD authoring until version 26.3 (June
2026), which removed the CD Layout view; it never wrote DDP. Audacity does batch processing,
through Macros, and has noise reduction and click removal; what it lacks is DDP, CD layout and a
mastering-grade loudness meter. Sound Forge Pro ran on the Mac until Sound Forge Pro Mac 3;
MAGIX stopped its development, and the line is Windows only.

## Sequoia

MAGIX's mastering and production workstation for Windows, the professional edition of
Samplitude. It adds to Samplitude what broadcasters, classical engineers and mastering houses
ask for: source-destination editing with four-point cuts, a CD and DDP mastering workflow with
PQ and ISRC editing, broadcast and network features, and very high channel counts. Its editing
is object-based: each clip on a track carries its own gain, fades, effects and plugins.

Samplitude began in 1992 on the Amiga, by SEK'D in Germany, and came to Windows in 1995; MAGIX
bought it in 1999 and built Sequoia on it from the early 2000s. Sequoia 17 is the current
version. In August 2025 Boris FX bought Sequoia, Samplitude and Music Studio from MAGIX. Its
strengths are album assembly, editing speed and a long reputation for its audio engine; its
limits are Windows only and a price well above the others.

- [Samplitude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samplitude), Wikipedia (Sequoia is covered there).
- [Magix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magix) and [Boris FX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_FX), Wikipedia.

## WaveLab

Steinberg's editing and mastering suite for Windows and macOS. It opens single files for
destructive editing, batches of files, and the **Audio Montage**, a non-destructive multitrack
assembly with per-clip effects that is the centre of its mastering workflow. It writes DDP,
CD and file sets, has extensive metering (EBU R 128 loudness, true peak, spectrum, phase), a
spectrum editor, batch processing with scripting, and from version 12 it hosts ARA 2 plugins
such as SpectraLayers and Melodyne inside the editor.

WaveLab was started in 1995 for Windows, largely the work of one programmer, Philippe Goutier,
and published by Steinberg (owned by Yamaha since 2004); it came to the Mac in 2010. WaveLab 12
came out in January 2024, in Pro, Elements and, for spoken word, Cast editions. Its strengths
are breadth and cross-platform licensing; its limits are the learning curve of so many
features and, for Pro, a price near the top of the range.

- [WaveLab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WaveLab) and [Steinberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steinberg), Wikipedia.
- [SpectraLayers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SpectraLayers), Wikipedia.

## Adobe Audition

Adobe's waveform and multitrack editor for Windows and macOS, part of Creative Cloud. It pairs
a destructive Waveform view with a non-destructive Multitrack view, and is strongest in
post-production: the Spectral Frequency Display for repair, the Essential Sound panel for
quick dialogue, music and effects treatment, Remix for refitting music to a length, and a direct
round trip with Premiere Pro.

Audition began as **Cool Edit**, a shareware editor by David Johnston's Syntrillium Software in
the mid-1990s; Adobe bought Cool Edit Pro in 2003 and renamed it. It moved to the Mac with CS5.5
(2011) and to subscription only with Creative Cloud (2013). Version 26.3 (June 2026) removed the
CD Layout view and CD authoring. Its strengths are video integration and repair tools; its
limits are the subscription and the lack of CD, DDP and album mastering tools.

- [Adobe Audition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adobe_Audition), Wikipedia.

## Sound Forge

A waveform editor for Windows known for speed and precise destructive editing of single files,
with recording, batch conversion, restoration and CD burning in its Pro edition, and Audio
Studio as a cheaper edition.

Monty Schmidt's Sonic Foundry of Madison, Wisconsin released it in 1992. Sony bought it with
Vegas and Acid in 2003, MAGIX in 2016, and Boris FX in March 2026; development stayed in Madison
and Germany. A Mac version, Sound Forge Pro Mac, reached version 3 before MAGIX stopped
developing it. Its strengths are a lean, fast workflow; its limits are Windows only and the
single-file focus, with no montage or album view of the kind WaveLab and Sequoia have.

- [Sound Forge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sound_Forge) and
  [Sonic Foundry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonic_Foundry), Wikipedia.

## Audacity

The best-known free and open-source audio editor, under the GPL, for Windows, macOS and Linux.
It records, edits multitrack projects, hosts VST3, LV2 and Audio Unit plugins with real-time
effects, runs batch jobs through Macros, and includes noise reduction, click removal and
loudness normalization.

Dominic Mazzoni and Roger Dannenberg began it at Carnegie Mellon University; the first release
was in May 2000. Muse Group took it over in 2021. **Audacity 4** (September 2026) is the largest
change in its history: a new Qt interface, non-destructive clip editing with overlapping clips,
workspaces and a new project format (.aup4), while dropping tempo tracks, LADSPA and Vamp
analyzers. Tenacity is a community fork. Its strengths are zero cost and reach; its limits are
the missing mastering tools (no DDP or CD layout, limited metering) and lighter restoration.

- [Audacity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audacity_(audio_editor)), Wikipedia.

## iZotope RX

The reference tool for audio repair, for Windows and macOS: a standalone spectral editor and a
set of plugins for denoising, declicking, de-humming, de-reverb, dialogue isolation and music
rebalancing, many now machine-learning based. It is not a mastering assembly tool, and many
engineers use it beside one of the editors above.

iZotope was founded in 2001 in Cambridge, Massachusetts; RX first appeared in 2007. iZotope
joined Native Instruments in 2023 and, when inMusic bought Native Instruments, went to Boris FX
in July 2026. RX 12 came out in April 2026, in Elements, Standard and Advanced editions.

- [iZotope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IZotope) and [Audio restoration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Audio_restoration), Wikipedia.

## Pyramix

Merging Technologies' workstation for Windows, used for classical recording, mastering and
high-resolution production. It records and edits DSD up to DSD256 and DXD (352.8 kHz), which
Merging developed for it in 2004, and handles SACD production, very high channel counts,
immersive formats and Ravenna/AES67 audio networking; source-destination editing and DDP are
central to it.

Merging was founded in 1990 in Puidoux, Switzerland, and has been part of Sennheiser (through
Neumann) since 2022. Pyramix 15 (late 2024) comes in Elements, Pro and Premium packs. Its
limits are Windows only and a price for professional studios.

- [Merging Technologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merging_Technologies), Wikipedia.
- [Digital eXtreme Definition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_eXtreme_Definition) and
  [Direct Stream Digital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct_Stream_Digital), Wikipedia.

## SADiE

A British editing and mastering system for Windows, long a standard in classical music and
radio: fast source-destination editing, CD and DDP mastering, and dedicated broadcast versions.
It was one of the first Windows-based workstations, around 1992, originally sold with its own
hardware and later as software; it is sold by Prism Sound, currently as SADiE 6. Its limits are
Windows only and a small market outside classical and broadcast.

- SADiE has no Wikipedia article of its own; it is listed in the
  [Comparison of digital audio editors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comparison_of_digital_audio_editors)
  and the history of the [Digital audio workstation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_audio_workstation), Wikipedia.

## Acoustica

Acon Digital's editor for Windows and macOS, from Oslo, Norway. The Standard edition covers
editing, mastering, restoration and sound-design processing; Premium adds spectral editing and
multichannel up to 7.1.2. Both have multitrack sessions, batch processing and audio CD
projects. It is often named as a lower-cost alternative to WaveLab and Sound Forge.

- [Acoustica (software)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acoustica_(software)), Wikipedia.

## Ocenaudio

A free (but closed-source) editor for Windows, macOS and Linux, developed in Brazil at the
Federal University of Santa Catarina. It is light and quick, previews effects in real time
before applying them, allows several selections at once and hosts VST plugins. It suits simple
editing and teaching rather than mastering.

- Ocenaudio has no Wikipedia article of its own; it appears in the
  [List of Linux audio software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Linux_audio_software), Wikipedia.

## Other editors

Several more are often used for editing and mastering. **Samplitude** is Sequoia's sibling for
music production. **SpectraLayers** (Steinberg) is a spectral editor in its own right.
**REAPER** (Cockos) is an inexpensive workstation that many use as an editor and mastering
host. **Hindenburg** is built for radio and podcast journalism. On the Mac, **DSP-Quattro**
offers CD mastering, and **Fission** (Rogue Amoeba) fast lossless trimming.

- [Samplitude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samplitude), [SpectraLayers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SpectraLayers)
  and [REAPER](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/REAPER), Wikipedia.
