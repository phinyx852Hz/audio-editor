# Bol Processor examples

`-gr.dhin--`, `-al.dhin--` and `-se.dhin--` are the grammar, alphabet and settings of a tablā
qāʿida of the Ajrada gharānā, "dhin--dhagena". This is the grammar that Jim Kippen and Bernard Bel
built in Bol Processor BP1, with rule weights inferred from real performances, as carried over to
BP3 in Bernard Bel's examples (github.com/bolprocessor/bp3-ctests, commit a34bd4a).

The qāʿida belongs to the Ajrada gharānā. PHI's owner learned it from Ustad Akram Khan, a direct
descendant of that lineage, and included it in PHI on that authority (2026-10-31). The grammar is
Kippen and Bel's work.

The alphabet's `*` homomorphism is the khālī: dha becomes ta, ge becomes ke and dhin becomes tin,
so the grammar can write a variation's closed half from its open half.

Open it with Compose > South and West Asia > Bol Processor... (docs/bol-processor.md).
